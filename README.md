# Adventure-Works-Analysis-
Data Source: Kaggle.com
I conducted an interactive Adventure Works business analysis using Power BI to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights. The analysis focused on understanding sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, and trends over time.
I cleaned and prepared the dataset before developing interactive visualizations, including KPI cards, charts, tables, slicers, a map, and a business dashboard. The analysis examined sales and profit by year, month, category, sub-category, country, and customer, while also identifying top-performing customers and products.
The dashboard enables users to interactively filter the data and explore key business trends, making it easier to identify high-performing product areas, changes in profitability, customer contribution, and opportunities for improved business performance.
Tools & Skills: Power BI, Data Cleaning, Data Analysis, Data Visualization, PivotTables, DAX, Interactive Dashboards, Slicers, KPI Analysis, and Business Intelligence.
